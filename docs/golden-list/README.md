# Golden List: Trusted Counterparties

The "Golden List" is the REC platform's repository of vetted, verified, and high-trust entities operating within the Iran-Russia trade corridor.

---

## 1. Governance & Verification
Every entity in the Golden List undergoes a mandatory multi-step vetting process:
- **Compliance Check:** Validated corporate registration (KYB), Tax Clearance, and Sanction screening.
- **Performance History:** Track record of successful trade execution, document accuracy, and reliability.
- **Bilateral Approval:** Verification by both the Iranian and Russian trade desks to ensure operational capability.

---

## 2. Categories

### A. Certified Suppliers (Iran)
- Producers and exporters of dates, pistachios, and agricultural inputs.
- Entities capable of delivering consistent quality (Certificate of Analysis - COA) and timely logistics.

### B. Strategic Buyers (Russia)
- Retail chains, wholesale distributors, and food processors in the Russian Federation.
- Entities vetted for financial solvency and import compliance (152-FZ).

### C. Logistics & Financial Partners
- **Logistics:** Freight forwarders with established corridors (e.g., Caspian Sea maritime routes).
- **Settlement:** Authorized banking entities and payment gateways (e.g., BRICS Pay-ready channels) ensuring secure currency clearing.

---

## 3. Maintenance
- The Golden List is subject to **periodic review (quarterly)**.
- Any entity failing to meet quality, compliance, or ethical standards is subject to suspension or permanent removal.
