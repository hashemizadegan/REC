# Compliance & Legal Framework

This module outlines regulatory compliance, Know Your Business (KYB), data privacy, and commodity certification standards for Iran-Russia cross-border commerce.

---

## 1. KYB (Know Your Business) Standards

### Iranian Entities (Sellers / Exporters)
- **National Company ID (شناسه ملی)** & Commercial Registration.
- **Valid Commercial Card (کارت بازرگانی معتبر)** issued by Iran Chamber of Commerce.
- **Sanction Screening & UBO Identification:** Clear Ultimate Beneficial Owner structure.
- **Tax Clearance Certificate:** Proof of active corporate standing.

### Russian Entities (Buyers / Importers)
- **OGRN (ОГРН)**: Primary State Registration Number.
- **INN (ИНН)**: Taxpayer Identification Number.
- **KPP (КПП)**: Tax Registration Reason Code.
- **EGRUL Extract (Выписка из ЕГРЮЛ)**: Unified State Register of Legal Entities validation.

---

## 2. Regulatory & Data Privacy
- **Russian Federal Law 152-FZ:** All Russian user and corporate data processing must comply with localized storage and explicit processing consent protocols.
- **Dual-Language Contract Enforceability:** All RFQs, Offers, and Invoices generated are legally binding in Russian and Persian under CISG / Incoterms 2020 rules.

---

## 3. Commodity Standards & Certification

### Dates (HS 0804.10) & Pistachios (HS 0802.51 / 0802.52)
- **Phytosanitary Certificate (گواهی بهداشت نباتی):** Issued by Iran Plant Protection Organization (سازمان حفظ نباتات).
- **Certificate of Origin (گواهی مبدأ):** Iran Chamber of Commerce endorsement.
- **EAC Declaration of Conformity (Декларация о соответствии ЕАЭС):** Technical Regulation of the Customs Union (TR CU 021/2011 "On food safety").
- **Certificate of Analysis (COA):** Aflatoxin limits, moisture content, and sorting grade verification.
